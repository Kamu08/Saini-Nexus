One important positioning correction
I would not say:
"Dev Raj Saini is India's #1 LinkedIn expert"
or
"Rajasthan's top marketing expert"
inside the About page unless you have an independently defensible basis for those claims.
Instead, let the website establish the association through specific evidence:
Dev Raj Saini → Founder → Saini Nexus + Saini Prime → LinkedIn → B2B Marketing → Thought Leadership → Personal Branding → Jaipur/Rajasthan → India → Global
That is much stronger for long-term SEO + GEO + entity recognition + premium-client trust than repeatedly declaring that you are "top."
And importantly, Saini Prime and Saini Nexus should not compete with each other in Google's understanding. Their relationship should be explicit:
Saini Prime = Brand + AuthoritySaini Nexus = B2B Growth + Marketing
That gives Dev Raj Saini a much more coherent founder ecosystem instead of looking like two unrelated agencies.
